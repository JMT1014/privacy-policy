MyWalks — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 December 2026
Applies to: MyWalks for Android (com.josesito.mywalks)

MyWalks is a walking tracker. It records your walks so you can look back at them. Everything it records stays on your phone.

There is no account to create, no sign-in, and no server that belongs to MyWalks. Nothing you record is uploaded to us, because there is no “us” to upload it to.

What MyWalks records

Location, while a walk is running. From the moment you tap Start My Walk until you tap Finish & Save, MyWalks reads your device’s GPS to draw the route you took and to work out how far and how fast you walked. It does not read your location before you start a walk, after you finish one, or while the app is closed or in the background.

Step count, while a walk is running. MyWalks reads your device’s built-in step counter during a walk so it can show how many steps that walk took. It cannot see steps from before or after the walk.

A photo, if you take one. When you finish a walk, MyWalks offers to take a photo. This is always optional. If you take one it is saved with that walk. If you skip it, nothing is recorded.

A first name, if you enter one. You can enter a first name in Settings so the app can greet you. It is used for nothing else. Leaving it blank is fine — the app greets you as “Walker”.

Your settings. Your chosen theme, units and milestone dates are saved so the app looks the same next time you open it.

What MyWalks does not record

  • No account, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • No advertising identifier, and no advertising.
  • No analytics, usage tracking or crash reporting.
  • No location outside of an active walk.
  • No microphone, and no access to your existing photo library.

Where it is kept

All of it is stored in MyWalks’ own private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it.

If you have Android’s backup feature switched on, your phone may copy this app’s data to your own Google account, in the same way it does for other apps. That backup belongs to you, and is controlled by your device settings, not by MyWalks.

The two services MyWalks contacts

MyWalks needs the internet for two things. Neither is sent your name or any identifier from the app.

Map tiles. To draw the map behind your route, MyWalks downloads map images for the area you are walking in. The map provider receives your IP address and the area being displayed, as any website would.

Weather. To show the current temperature, MyWalks sends your approximate coordinates to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) and receives a temperature back. No name, account or device identifier is included.

These services have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

Deleting your data

  • One walk: swipe it away on the My Story tab. Its photo is deleted with it.
  • All photos, keeping the walks: Settings → Walk photos → Delete all photos.
  • All walks: Settings → Delete all walks. This also removes their photos and your earned milestones.
  • Everything: uninstall MyWalks. All walks, photos and settings are removed with the app.

Because nothing is stored on a server, there is nothing left behind anywhere else once you have done this.

Children

MyWalks is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
